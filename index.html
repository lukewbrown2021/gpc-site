About

A focused, deal-by-deal approach.

Rather than raising a blind-pool fund, Growth Partner Capital sources individual opportunities first and then brings together a small group of investors for each one. Our partners see the full picture before committing and choose the deals they want to be part of.

Our recent vehicles have funded growth in industrial and transportation technology, backing businesses with technology already proven in the field as they expand into new markets.

How We Work

Typical deal structure.

Each investment is organized in its own vehicle with terms built around the opportunity, aligning our interests with those of our partners.

01

02

03
